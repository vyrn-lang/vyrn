#### A write into a second name for a parameter is refused as a take is, and a copy is `.copy()` (2026-09-25, `m7-copy`)
RFC-0125, milestone M7.
Decision: the lead chose to refuse a store into a part of a `let mut` bound to a parameter, and a `modify` argument of one. The rule is the take's own predicate (`kind_of_borrow`, the `param_take` path) with its sentence family. `std/tw` writes `.copy()`, which is the existing `@copy` row. The brief's premise, the arm's deep copy, did not hold: the arm copied the layout's bytes.
Went: `twCssNothingAfter`'s refusal at the name screen. `let mut s = sc.copy()` is built by `copy_stack` on both walks and judged a fresh owner. The first commit (an `impl Copy` receiver is a call to the declaration, with its type arguments) moves the `slots` manifest row.
Stayed: `slots` `main`, at `for p in people` and `words[w] = ..`, element projections of a user container. `mapkey` `main`, at `read m[key p]` of a record or enum key. Neither is a copy.
Rejected: the widened form, every write into any borrow through the take's predicate.
