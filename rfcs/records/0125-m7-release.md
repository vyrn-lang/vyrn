#### A released value's boxes are its caller's, and the core's switch row says so (2026-09-25, `m7-release`)
RFC-0125, milestone M7.
Decision: A, the lead's, on this track's paragraph. The core's row states the convention both walks already used. In a declared release, a switch on the receiver, or on its temporary, is consuming, so the binders own the parts, and it owns no box. The caller frees the boxes after the release returns (RFC-0096, `emit_rel`'s `Rel::Call`). `@t2 = self` is the receiver's place under a second name, not a copy. B, where the release frees its own boxes, was rejected: it changes the convention of every declared release, and the 12 that never take `self` apart would leak their boxes with no judgment to refuse it.
