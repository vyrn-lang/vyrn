# RFC-0126 — a type constructor is a case in every pass

- **Status:** Census, closed (2026-09-04). No collapse is taken. The census is
  the deliverable, it is pinned by `compiler/vyrn-cli/tests/surface.rs`, and the
  ranked collapses in §5 each say what they would cost. One defect the census
  found is fixed: `Type::Stream`'s doc comment in `ast.rs` claimed a lowering
  the code stopped using at RFC-0075 M2b (§6).
- **Depends on:** RFC-0125 §2.8, which deferred this census and named its
  candidates; RFC-0082 (containers are Vyrn — the array and Map collapses were
  already attempted, and the numbers that stopped them are quoted here);
  RFC-0091 M4 (the `SmallArray` port, stopped a third time); RFC-0056, RFC-0028,
  RFC-0075, RFC-0083, RFC-0085 M4a and RFC-0079, which are what the rows cite.
- **Evidence:** the counts in §3, measured at this branch's tip by the method in
  §2 and recomputed by `tests/surface.rs` on every run; the layout table in
  `vyrn-codegen/src/lib.rs`'s `llt_of`; `vyrn-frontend/src/types.rs`'s `resolve`;
  RFC-0082's own gate numbers.

---

## 1. The question

RFC-0125 §2.8 wrote the surface out of its own scope and said what it thought
was there:

> **The surface census.** Three array kinds, three Map key kinds, and several
> result-like types can become library over fixed arrays and canonical packs.
> Each is a checker case that becomes a desugar. A later RFC, done while the
> compiler is still in Rust and easy to change.

This is that RFC. It measures every constructor of `ast::Type`, one row each,
and gives each a verdict. The paragraph above is treated as a hypothesis, not as
a finding, because two of its three candidates have already been tried and
stopped with numbers on the record.

§1.1 of RFC-0125 says the size is `(surface × types × builtins × engines)`. The
`types` factor is 35 constructors. This census prices that factor.

---

## 2. The method, so a number can be re-derived

A constructor's cost is measured as the number of times its name appears as
`Type::<Name>` in the seven files that carry the language's decisions. The rule
is mechanical, and `tests/surface.rs` applies exactly it:

1. A line whose trimmed text starts with `//` is skipped, so a doc comment that
   names a constructor is not a case.
2. An item annotated `#[cfg(test)]` is skipped whole — from that line to the
   line closing the first brace opened at or after it. A fixture in a test
   module is not a pass.
3. In what is left, `Type::<Name>` is counted where the next character is not a
   letter, digit or underscore. That is what keeps `Type::Int` from counting
   `Type::IntN`, and `Type::Array` from counting `Type::ArrayN`.

**A mention, not an arm.** A `match` arm, a construction and a `matches!` each
count as one. Counting arms alone would need a Rust parser and would
under-report: a constructor the checker *builds* in twelve places is stated
twelve times, and each is a place a collapse has to change. The unit is
therefore "the constructor is named here".

The seven files, and why each:

| column | file | what it decides |
|---|---|---|
| `checker` | `vyrn-frontend/src/checker.rs` | what a program means |
| `interp` | `vyrn-frontend/src/interp.rs` | the interpreter's engine |
| `native` | `vyrn-codegen/src/lib.rs` | the textual-IR emitter, and `llt_of` |
| `wasm` | `vyrn-codegen/src/direct.rs` | the direct wasm emitter |
| `types` | `vyrn-frontend/src/types.rs` | `resolve`, `substitute`, `solve_param` |
| `prelude` | `vyrn-frontend/src/prelude.rs` | the seeded builtin signatures |
| `editor` | `vyrn-frontend/src/symbols.rs` | the per-type method and hover tables |

`prelude` and `editor` together are the "builtin rows that exist only for this
constructor" column the census was asked for. They are counted the same way as
the rest rather than as rows, for one reason: a row is not a unit anybody can
compare across files, and one metric that means the same thing in seven places
is worth more than two metrics that each mean something else in one.

**What this leaves out on purpose.** `own.rs`, `movecheck.rs`, `parser.rs`,
`codec.rs`, `loader.rs` and the lowered form add about a thousand more mentions.
They are downstream of the seven: no collapse can delete a case there that it
does not first delete here, so the seven are the ones that decide, and adding
the rest would move every row by roughly the same factor without changing an
order.

### 3.1 What the `interp` column is not

The interpreter's column is small everywhere, and it does not mean the
interpreter is cheap. The interpreter has no types at runtime: its cost is in
`Val`, and `Val` has 23 variants of its own. Read that way the column is the
most useful one in the table, because the interpreter is the one engine that has
already answered §2.8's questions by building a representation:

| `Type` constructors | the `Val` they become | what that says |
|---|---|---|
| `Array`, `ArrayN`, `SmallArray` | `Val::Array` | the three array kinds ARE one at runtime, in one engine |
| `Map` | `Val::Map`, `Val::MapI`, `Val::MapU` | one constructor is THREE representations (RFC-0117) |
| `Stream` | `Val::Stream` | not `Val::Array` since RFC-0075 M2b — see §6 |
| `Fn`, `Lazy` | `Val::Fn` | `resolve` already answers `Fn([], T)` for a `lazy T` |
| `Named`, `App`, `Omit`, `Pick`, `Merge`, `Partial` | whatever they resolve to | no representation of their own |
| `ConstInt`, `Never`, `Err`, `Param`, `Task`, `Logger` | none | a compile-time constructor, or another `Val` |

So §2.8's two headline candidates point in opposite directions. The three array
kinds are already ONE representation in the interpreter and three in the
compiled backends. The three Map key kinds are ONE constructor and three
representations everywhere. A census of the surface prices the first; the second
is not a surface question at all.

### 3.2 The layout is already stated once

`llt_of` in `vyrn-codegen/src/lib.rs` is one `match` from a type to an LLVM
shape, and `layout::of_ll` parses what it prints, so the direct wasm backend
reads that table rather than keeping one. The census agrees: no constructor has
a second layout statement in `direct.rs`. RFC-0101 M5's work is holding. What
the two compiled backends still duplicate is the OPERATIONS on a shape, not the
shape.

## 4. The verdict, one row each

`stays` — the constructor is irreducible, or a measurement already refused the
collapse. `desugar` — another constructor already expresses it and the compiler
computes that expansion today. `decide` — a collapse exists and something has to
be chosen first; the row says what.

| constructor | what it is | RFC | verdict | the desugar, or the reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `Type::Int` | the 64-bit signed default | RFC-0002 | stays | `IntN{64,true}` is never built — the parser maps `Int64` here — so the merge renames 182 sites and removes no rule |
| `Type::IntN` | a sized integer | RFC-0002 | stays | four widths and two signs in one constructor: it is already the collapse |
| `Type::Float` | 64-bit IEEE-754 | RFC-0002 | stays | the default float, as `Int` is the default integer |
| `Type::Float32` | 32-bit IEEE-754 | RFC-0002 | decide | `FloatN{bits}` would do to floats what `IntN` did to integers; decide first whether the two widths differ by more than a width at the coercion ladder |
| `Type::F32x4` | four `Float32` lanes | RFC-0083 | stays | a value with its own representation and its own opcodes |
| `Type::I32x4` | four `Int32` lanes | RFC-0083 | stays | no divide, no sqrt, and it gains the bitwise operators — a different operation set, not a different lane |
| `Type::F64x2` | two `Float64` lanes | RFC-0083 | stays | the width explicit SIMD exists for |
| `Type::Mask32x4` | four `Bool` lanes | RFC-0083 | decide | one `Mask{lanes}` constructor saves 43 mentions; RFC-0083 argued that the SURFACE should not spell `Mask<N>`, and said nothing about the AST |
| `Type::Mask64x2` | two `Bool` lanes | RFC-0083 | decide | the row above, and the same decision; the two are one |
| `Type::Bool` | a boolean | RFC-0002 | stays | `i1`, and the type every condition has |
| `Type::Str` | a UTF-8 string | RFC-0002 | decide | it could be a validated `Array<UInt8>` — RFC-0082's boundary allows it, since a String owns its buffer — but it is 242 mentions, every literal and interpolation, and all of `std/strings`; decide whether the UTF-8 invariant is a validated type before pricing the port |
| `Type::Unit` | no value | RFC-0002 | stays | `void`, and what every statement returns |
| `Type::Named` | a declared type by name | RFC-0003 | stays | `resolve` expands it, but a recursive declaration has no finite expansion, so the name has to survive |
| `Type::Option` | `Option<T>` | RFC-0005 | decide | it is `enum { None, Some(T) }` and could be `Type::Enum`; the layouts differ (`{ i1, i64, i64 }` against `{ i64, i64… }`), and `?`, `if let` and every optional projection are typed on it — decide whether the two-word payload earns a constructor |
| `Type::Result` | `Result<T, E>` | RFC-0005 | decide | the row above, and the same layout; the two are one decision |
| `Type::Record` | a structural record | RFC-0002 | stays | the constructor the four transformers desugar INTO |
| `Type::Omit` | `Omit<T, f…>` | RFC-0002 | desugar | `Type::Record` — `types::resolve` computes it already; §5.2 says what stops the deletion |
| `Type::Pick` | `Pick<T, f…>` | RFC-0002 | desugar | `Type::Record`, the same way |
| `Type::Merge` | `Merge<A, B>` | RFC-0002 | desugar | `Type::Record`, the same way |
| `Type::Partial` | `Partial<T>` | RFC-0002 | desugar | `Type::Record` with each field wrapped in `Option` |
| `Type::Enum` | a sum type | RFC-0002 | stays | the constructor `Option` and `Result` would desugar into |
| `Type::Param` | a generic parameter | RFC-0002 | stays | substituted at each call site; nothing else names an unknown |
| `Type::App` | `Box<Int>` | RFC-0002 | stays | `resolve` substitutes it, and a recursive generic has no finite expansion — `Named`'s reason one level up |
| `Type::Array` | a growable heap array | RFC-0002 | stays | RFC-0082: you cannot write the thing that allocates memory in terms of itself |
| `Type::ArrayN` | `Array<T, N>`, a value aggregate | RFC-0002 | stays | the type of an array literal, and the base §2.8 wants everything else written over |
| `Type::SmallArray` | `SmallArray<T, N>` | RFC-0056 | stays | measured: RFC-0082 "Where the thesis stops" — inline capacity is uninitialized memory, three language features are missing, and the nearest expressible container ran 1.8x slower on a push, 7.9x slower on an indexed read, with 13% more wasm |
| `Type::ConstInt` | the `8` in a type argument | RFC-0056 | decide | the parser could read the literal at the type-argument site and never build the node; then its two refusals become parse errors, and a parse error stops the file where a checker error accumulates (RFC-0006) — decide whether those two diagnostics may become fatal |
| `Type::Map` | `Map<K, V>` | RFC-0028 | stays | measured: RFC-0082 M3 withdrew Map over Array at 18x on the interpreter. The three KEY kinds are a representation split, not a surface one — the constructor is already one |
| `Type::Stream` | a linear sequence | RFC-0075 | stays | not `Array` any more: since M2b it is a six-word tagged header over two producers, with a `Val::Stream` of its own (§6) |
| `Type::Task` | a task handle | RFC-0025 | stays | `spawn` produces it and `join` consumes it; erasing it erases the obligation to join |
| `Type::Logger` | a logger handle | RFC-0008 | decide | eleven mentions and five methods over what is a pointer to a name string; `impl` blocks make a library `Logger` expressible (RFC-0080, RFC-0084) — decide where RFC-0008's level filtering lives if the five methods become ordinary functions |
| `Type::Fn` | a function value | RFC-0023 | stays | defunctionalized to `{ tag, payload }` (RFC-0037); the constructor `Lazy` desugars into |
| `Type::Lazy` | a deferred field | RFC-0085 | desugar | `Type::Fn([], T)` — `types::resolve` answers exactly that already; §5.1 says what stops the deletion |
| `Type::Never` | the bottom type | RFC-0079 | stays | `assignable(Never, _)` holds and the reverse does not; no other constructor has that rule |
| `Type::Err` | the recovery sentinel | RFC-0006 | stays | 138 of its 141 mentions are the checker carrying on after an error it already reported |

Of the 35 rows, 5 say `desugar`, 8 say `decide` and 22 say `stays`.

## 5. The ranked collapses, and why none is taken

Cheapest first. "Saved" is the census total for the constructors deleted.

### 5.1 `Type::Lazy` into `Type::Fn([], T)` — 21 saved, and the marker does not fit

`resolve` already answers `Fn([], T)` for a `lazy T`, so no engine has a layout,
an ownership rule or a dispatcher for it. The constructor survives only as a
MARKER: `lazy` is legal in field position and nowhere else, and three passes
must still see it — the field read, which forces; the codec, which forces too;
and reflection.

The marker's only other carrier is `ast::Field`, a two-field struct built in
**246 places** across the workspace. Adding a third field edits every one of
them, or hides them behind a constructor and a `Default`. That diff is larger
than the 21 mentions it removes, and it moves a fact out of the type that
carries it into a struct that does not. **Not taken, and it should not be taken
until `Field` grows a constructor for some other reason.**

### 5.2 `Omit`/`Pick`/`Merge`/`Partial` into `Type::Record` — 69 saved, blocked by generics

The four are pure functions of a field list, `resolve` computes each, and no
engine gives any of them a representation: `llt_of` answers `void` and its own
comment calls the arm unreachable. So the move is to compute them ONCE, in the
loader, after the type table exists, and delete every downstream arm.

It does not work, and the blocker is one line in `resolve`: the expansion needs
the base's field list, and a transformer over a bare type parameter has none —
`fields_d` answers `None` and the result is `Type::Unit`. A generic declaration
(`type P<T> = Partial<T>`) therefore cannot be folded until it is instantiated,
which is what `resolve` at each use already is.

**The gate this needs, stated so a later slice can take it:** refuse a
transformer over a bare type parameter, with a diagnostic, and the loader's fold
becomes total. The corpus does not write one today —
`examples/utility.vyrn` is the only file that uses a transformer at all, and its
three uses all name a concrete type. That is a language decision, not a
refactor, so it is not taken here.

### 5.3 `Type::ConstInt` into the type-argument parse — 7 saved, a worse first error

Seven mentions, and the node exists only to carry `N` from the parser to
`SmallArray`. The parser could read the literal at the type-argument site and
never build a node. Its two refusals — `Box<3>`, and a bare integer in a type
position nothing consumes — would then be parse errors. RFC-0006's accumulation
is what that costs: a checker error reports and continues, a parse error stops
the file. Seven mentions do not buy a worse first error. **Not taken.**

### 5.4 `Float`/`Float32` into `FloatN{bits}` — 154 touched, 0 saved

A rename, not a collapse: every arm gains a width guard and no rule is deleted.
`IntN` did this and kept `Int` separate for the same reason. **Not taken.**

### 5.5 `Option`/`Result` into `Type::Enum` — 213 saved, and it changes bytes

The largest real prize in the table, and the one with the most rules attached.
Both lower to `{ i1, i64, i64 }`; a user enum lowers to `{ i64, i64… }` with one
payload slot per the widest variant. The collapse changes the tag width and the
payload count in both compiled backends and both `Val` variants in the
interpreter, and `?`, `if let`, refutable `let`, `tryField` and every optional
projection are typed against the constructors. That is a milestone, not a slice.
**Not taken here, and recorded as the biggest one.**

### 5.6 The two §2.8 named that are already refused

`SmallArray` and `Map` are not open questions. RFC-0082 M3 withdrew Map over
Array at 18x on the interpreter. RFC-0091 M4 resumed the `SmallArray` port and
stopped: three missing language features, and the nearest expressible container
measured 1.8x and 7.9x slower with 13% more wasm. RFC-0082 states the boundary
in its own words — a container whose spare capacity is a heap buffer it owns can
be Vyrn, a container whose spare capacity is inline cannot — and `SmallArray` is
on the far side of it.

**So §2.8's paragraph is two-thirds wrong, and saying so is what this census was
for.** "Three array kinds" is one measured refusal (`SmallArray`), one
irreducible primitive (`Array`) and one base that everything else would be
written over (`ArrayN`). "Three Map key kinds" is not a surface question at all:
it is one constructor and three representations. The third — "several
result-like types" — is real, is §5.5, and is the most expensive item in the
table.

## 6. The defect the census found

`ast.rs`'s doc comment for `Type::Stream` said the lowering is `Array<T>`'s
exactly, in all three engines: `{ ptr data, i64 len, i64 cap }`, `Val::Array`,
and the same indexed walk for `for … in`. RFC-0075 M2b changed all three and the
comment did not follow. `llt_of` answers `{ ptr, i64, i64, i64, i64, i64 }` — a
six-word tagged header over two producers — and the interpreter has a
`Val::Stream` holding a `StreamVal` of its own. A reader pricing the Stream
collapse from that comment would have priced a free one. The comment now says
what the code does, and cites M2b for the change.

RFC-0125 §1.6 already records the general form: about 30% of every large file is
prose that retells an RFC, and prose does not compile.

---

## 7. What this RFC does not decide

- **Whether any collapse should be taken.** Six rows say `decide` and each names
  its decision. None is taken here, and a later slice that takes one has this
  table to move.
- **The `Val` census.** §3.1 maps the constructors onto the interpreter's 23
  variants but does not price them. The interpreter is what RFC-0125 §2.7
  deletes, so pricing it belongs to that milestone.
- **The thousand mentions outside the seven files.** §2 says why they are left
  out and why leaving them out changes no order.
